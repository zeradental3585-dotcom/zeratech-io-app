FAQ

Common questions

What should a coaching institute website have?
Course and batch pages, fees (or a 'get fee details' button), faculty, results and toppers, photos of the centre, a Google Map, an enquiry form and a WhatsApp button. A notice board for new batches and exam updates helps students return to the site.
How much does a coaching website cost?
Starter ₹9,999 (up to 5 pages, live in 7 days), Business ₹19,999 (up to 12 pages, Hindi + English, notice board from a Google Sheet) and Pro ₹34,999 (online registration and fee payment). Domain and first-year hosting are included. Prices exclude 18% GST (GST invoice provided). Use our website cost calculator for your exact price.
Can students enquire directly on WhatsApp?
Yes. Every course page gets a WhatsApp button that opens a chat with a message already written, so the student only has to press send.
Will my institute show up on Google?
We set up page titles, Google Search Console, your Google Business Profile and pages for each course and area — for example 'NEET coaching in Patna' or 'spoken English classes in Purnia' — so students searching nearby can find you.
Can I update batch timings and results myself?
Yes. We can connect simple sections like notices, batches and results to a Google Sheet, so you update the sheet and the website changes.
Is the website available in Hindi?
Yes. We build in Hindi, English or both. See our Hindi page.
