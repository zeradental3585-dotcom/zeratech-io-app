Can patients book appointments online?
Yes. We add an appointment request form and a WhatsApp button so patients can send a preferred date and time; your staff confirm it.
Will my clinic show up on Google Maps?
We set up or improve your Google Business Profile, link it to your website and build pages for your treatments and area, which helps you appear for searches like 'skin specialist near me'.
Can you show my Google reviews?
Yes. We can link to and display your real Google reviews. We never write fake reviews or testimonials.
Do you follow medical advertising rules?
We write factual, professional content about your qualifications, services and timings, and avoid exaggerated claims or guarantees of results. You review and approve all text before launch.
