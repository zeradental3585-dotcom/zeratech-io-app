      FAQ

      Common questions

      How much does a website cost in India in 2026?
      A small-business website typically costs ₹3,000–15,000 from cheap template sellers, ₹10,000–40,000 from freelancers, and ₹50,000–1,50,000+ from agencies. DIY builders cost ₹6,000–18,000 every year. Our custom, hand-coded packages are ₹9,999, ₹19,999 and ₹34,999 (plus 18% GST) with domain and first-year hosting included.
      How much does a small business website cost in the US or UK?
      Typical 2026 ranges are $200–1,000 for DIY builders, $1,500–8,000 for freelancers and $5,000–25,000 for small agencies. Our websites start at $249, with business sites at $599 and booking or e-commerce sites at $1,299.
      Is GST included in the calculator price?
      No. Indian prices are shown before 18% GST; the calculator shows the total with GST underneath. You receive a GST invoice. International (USD) prices have no Indian GST.
      Is the calculator price final?
      It is our published price for what you selected. We confirm it in a fixed written quote after a short chat. The quote only changes if you change the scope.
      What does a website cost every month after launch?
      Hosting and a domain cost about ₹3,000–8,000 a year in India if you manage them yourself. Our Care plan is ₹999/month (or $39/month internationally) and covers hosting, backups, security and small edits.
      Why is your price lower than agencies?
      We are a small studio with no sales team or office overheads, we hand-code on fast, low-cost hosting, and we reuse our own tested components. You get custom work without paying for an agency's overheads.
