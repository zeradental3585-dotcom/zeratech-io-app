Why Patna businesses need a proper website now

Patna is Bihar's biggest market for coaching, healthcare and education. Students from across the state come to the city for competitive-exam preparation, and families from smaller districts travel in for hospitals and specialists. Most of them search on a phone before they decide where to go.

Many Patna businesses still depend on a Facebook page, a Justdial listing or word of mouth. A clear website with your timings, services, fees and a WhatsApp button gives you a place on Google that you control — and works for visitors who search in Hindi as well as English.

We are from Bihar and build Hindi-first websites every day. Our own BighaWala.com serves land information for all 38 districts of Bihar, and KosiEducationTimes.com covers Bihar Board results and government jobs.
