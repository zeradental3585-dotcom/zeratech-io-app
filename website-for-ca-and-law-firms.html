FAQ

Common questions

Are CA firms and advocates allowed to have websites?
Professional bodies have rules about advertising and solicitation. ICAI and the Bar Council of India publish guidelines on what a member's website may contain. We build factual, information-style websites and you confirm the content follows your body's current guidelines before launch.
What pages does a CA firm website need?
Services (GST, income tax, audit, company registration, accounting), partners and team, office location, a client enquiry form, and useful pages such as due-date calendars that bring repeat visits.
Can clients upload documents?
We can add a secure document request form or link to a tool you already use. Tell us your workflow and we'll suggest the simplest safe option.
