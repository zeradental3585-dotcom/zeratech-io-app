FAQ

Common questions

What is white-label web development?
We build the website; your agency delivers it to your client under your own brand. Your client never needs to know we exist, and you keep the relationship and the margin.
Will you contact my clients?
No. We work only with you. No Zera branding goes on client sites unless you ask for it.
Do you sign an NDA?
Yes, on request, before you share any client details.
How do we communicate across time zones?
By email or your Slack/Trello/Asana, with written updates at each milestone. India (IST, UTC+5:30) overlaps with UK and Gulf working hours, and US/Canada/Australia clients can schedule calls in their morning or evening. See how we work.
How is pricing set?
Each project gets a fixed USD quote before work starts, based on our standard packages: Starter $249, Business $599, Growth $1,299. Monthly SEO from $129 can also be white-labelled so you resell recurring retainers.
Who owns the code?
Once paid, your agency (or your client) owns everything: code, design files, content and logins.

Have a project waiting?

Email the brief and get a fixed quote back. Mention your deadline and we'll tell you honestly if we can meet it.
