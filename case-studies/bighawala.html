Building a Hindi land-information portal for all 38 Bihar districts

A product built and run by Zera Technologies. Visit bighawala.com →

The problem

Land records in Bihar are spread across many portals — Apna Khata, Dakhil Kharij, Bhulekh, LPC — each with its own process. Many people either pay an agent to navigate them or give up.

What we built

BighaWala.com brings that scattered information into one Hindi-first site: a bigha/katha/dhur unit calculator, a land-cost and registry-fee calculator, step-by-step guides for dakhil kharij (mutation), apna khata, jamabandi and LPC applications, and land-rate pages for all 38 districts of Bihar.

Why it works

Each page targets a specific search the way people actually type it — in Hindi. The calculators give visitors a reason to stay and share, and every official process links to the correct government portal instead of trying to replace it.

The result

One free site now covers land-rate information for every district in the state. BighaWala.com has served 5,00,000+ users with 65+ pages and guides and 8 free tools, and is approved for Google AdSense.

What this means for your project

For a local business, the same approach means pages written in your customers' language, answering the exact questions they search for.
