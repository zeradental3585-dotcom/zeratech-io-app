Building Bihar's #1 Land Information Portal

Regional utility platform38 districtsHindi-first

Land records in Bihar involve a maze of portals: Apna Khata, Dakhil Kharij, Bhulekh, LPC, and more, each with its own workflow. Most residents either pay an agent to navigate it or give up entirely.

What we built

BighaWala.com brings that scattered information into one Hindi-first destination: a bigha/katha/dhur unit calculator, a land-cost and registry-fee calculator, and step-by-step guides for dakhil kharij (mutation), apna khata, jamabandi, and LPC applications, plus land-rate pages for all 38 districts of Bihar.

Why it works

Every page targets a specific, high-intent search query in the way people actually phrase it, in Hindi. The calculators give visitors a reason to stay and share the tool, and every official process links back to the correct government portal rather than trying to replace it.

The result

A single site now covers land-rate information for every district in the state, something no free government resource does in one place, positioned as Bihar's free, no-hidden-fee alternative to paid agents.
