A Bilingual Content Network From Scratch

Content network6 citiesHindi + English

Parenting advice online is either generic and Western-centric, or written only in English, missing a huge share of Indian parents who search in Hindi or Hinglish.

What we built

SafalBalak.com covers parenting, child development, and education topics in both Hindi and English, including a dedicated guide series for nursery school admissions in six major cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai, each covering the local RTE quota rules, timelines, and school options.

Why it works

City-specific admission guides are exactly the kind of content parents search for once a year, at a very specific, urgent moment. Publishing them in both languages doubles the addressable audience without doubling the topics.

The result

A structured content network with parallel English and Hindi article paths, growth guides, and tools, built to keep expanding city-by-city and topic-by-topic.
