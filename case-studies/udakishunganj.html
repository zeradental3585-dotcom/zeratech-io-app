Rebuilding a civic portal with Hindi at the root — without losing SEO

A product built and run by Zera Technologies. Visit udakishunganj.com →

The starting point

Udakishunganj.com launched as an English-language civic information site for Udakishunganj block in Madhepura district, covering the BDO office, ration cards, Anganwadi services and the panchayat directory.

The problem

Udakishunganj is an almost entirely Hindi-speaking area. An English-first homepage didn't match how the audience reads and searches, even though the content was accurate.

What we changed

We built a full Hindi version, using the three common local spellings of the place name (उदाकिशुनगंज, किशुनगंज, उदा किशुनगंज) in headings, meta descriptions and schema.org alternateName. Hindi content moved to the root domain, English to /en/, with hreflang tags, updated sitemaps and 301 redirects for every old URL.

The result

The default experience now matches the audience's language, the English version stays fully available, and no links were broken in the move.

What this means for your project

If you're redesigning or moving your own website, this is how we protect the Google rankings you've already earned.
