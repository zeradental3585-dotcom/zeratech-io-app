Website maintenance cost in India: what's included and what you should pay

Short answer: a basic website care plan in India costs ₹1,000–5,000 a month. For that you should get hosting and domain renewals, backups, security updates, uptime monitoring and small edits. Anything with content and SEO work costs more. Here's what each level includes — and what happens if nobody looks after your site.

What a good maintenance plan should include

  • Renewals handled for you — domain, hosting and SSL never expire by accident.
  • Backups you can restore from, kept off the main server.
  • Security and software updates — especially important on WordPress sites with plugins.
  • Uptime monitoring — someone knows the site is down before your customers do.
  • Small edits — new prices, timings, offers, photos, notices.
  • Google Business Profile posts — keep your Maps listing active (how to set one up).
  • Fresh content — new pages or articles that bring Google traffic.
  • A simple monthly report — visits, enquiries, what was done.

What happens if nobody maintains your website

  1. The domain expires and the site disappears — sometimes someone else buys the name.
  2. Information goes stale — old fees, old timings, an old phone number. Visitors leave or call the wrong number.
  3. The site gets hacked through an outdated plugin and Google shows a warning.
  4. Forms silently stop working and enquiries never reach you.
  5. Google traffic slowly drops because nothing new is ever added.

Do you really need a plan?

  • Yes, if your website is how customers find you, you change prices or timings often, or nobody on your team is technical.
  • Maybe not, if your site is a simple static page that rarely changes and you are comfortable renewing the domain and hosting yourself every year.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does website maintenance cost in India?
Basic care plans usually cost ₹1,000–5,000 a month. Plans with regular content updates, performance work and reports cost ₹5,000–15,000 a month, and e-commerce or complex sites ₹15,000–40,000+. Pay-as-you-go support is often ₹800–2,500 an hour.
What is included in website maintenance?
At minimum: hosting and domain renewals, SSL, regular backups, security and software updates, uptime monitoring and small text or photo changes. Better plans add Google Business Profile posts, new SEO content and a monthly report.
What is an AMC for a website?
An AMC (annual maintenance contract) is a maintenance plan paid yearly instead of monthly, often with a small discount for paying upfront.
Do I need maintenance for a small static website?
A hand-coded static site needs far less than a WordPress site with many plugins, but it still needs domain and hosting renewals, backups, SSL and someone to update prices, timings and offers. Out-of-date information costs more enquiries than most technical problems.
Can I cancel a maintenance plan?
With us, yes — plans are month to month. You keep your domain, code and content.
