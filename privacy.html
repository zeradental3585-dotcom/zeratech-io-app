Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026.

Zera Technologies ("we", "us") operates zeratech.io. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email, phone number, country, service of interest, budget range, and any message you include. This information is stored in a private Google Sheet and used only to respond to your inquiry.

Currency display

To show pricing in your local currency, this site makes a request to a third-party IP geolocation service and a currency exchange rate service. These requests only determine an approximate country and current exchange rates; we do not store this data ourselves.

Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's session storage to temporarily remember your detected currency during a visit, so pricing doesn't need to reload on every page. This data is cleared when you close your browser tab.

Third parties

We do not sell or share your contact information with third parties. Analytics, where used, are aggregated and do not identify individual visitors personally.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@zeratech.io.
