Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

zeratech.io is a property of and is operated by Zera Technologies ("we", "us"), Madhepura, Bihar, India. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit our quote or audit form, we collect what you enter: name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, country, service of interest, budget range, website address and your message. This is stored in a private Google Sheet and used only to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.

When you click a WhatsApp or email link, a message is prefilled with the name and address of the page you were on, so we know what you were looking at. You can edit or delete this text before sending.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — for example which pages are viewed and which contact buttons are clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and reports aggregated data. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your contact information. Data is processed by the services we use to run the site (Google Sheets, Google Analytics, our web host) under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and for our business records. Ask us any time to delete your details.
