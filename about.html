About Zera Technologies

We're a small, engineering-first studio that builds and grows digital products for ourselves and for clients — using the same playbook for both.

Why we exist

Most agencies show you a portfolio of screenshots. We'd rather show you the traffic. Every service we sell — web development, app development, and SEO — is something we run continuously on our own network of live products, from a 38-district Bihar land-information portal to bilingual parenting content and hyperlocal Hindi civic guides.

That means when we tell a client what will and won't work for their SEO, it isn't theory. It's a decision we've already made, and lived with, on our own sites.

How we work with clients

We take on a mix of in-house properties and client projects — including commissioned business websites like ZeraDental.com. Whether it's our product or yours, the standard is the same: fast, mobile-first, and built to be found on search without paying for ads.

What we believe

  • Organic first. We don't buy traffic, on our own sites or our clients'. Everything is engineered to earn search visibility.
  • Bilingual by default. Several of our own products serve Hindi-speaking audiences as the primary language, not an afterthought.
  • Real proof over portfolios. Every property in our portfolio is a live link you can click right now.
  • Engineering over templates. We build for speed, structure, and search — not just visual polish.
