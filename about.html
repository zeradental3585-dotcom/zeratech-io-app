About Zera Technologies

A small, engineering-first web studio that builds and grows digital products — for ourselves first, and then for clients, using the same playbook.

Who we are

Zera Technologies is a small, founder-led studio based in Madhepura, Bihar. When you work with us, you work directly with the person who designs, codes and optimises your site — no sales team, no outsourcing chain.

Why we exist

Most agencies show screenshots. We'd rather show working products. Every service we sell — web development, app development and SEO — is something we run on our own network of live sites: a land-information portal covering all 38 districts of Bihar, a bilingual parenting site, a Hindi-first civic portal, an education and job-alert site, financial calculators and a file-conversion app.

So when we tell you what will and won't work for your website, it isn't theory. It's a decision we have already made, and lived with, on our own sites.

What we believe

  • Organic first. Our own sites grow through search, not paid ads. We build yours the same way.
  • Bilingual by default. Several of our products serve Hindi-speaking audiences as the main language, not an afterthought.
  • Real proof only. We never invent testimonials or client logos. Our portfolio is our own live work.
  • Lean and fast. Hand-written code and low-cost hosting keep your site quick and cheap to run.

Who we work with

Small businesses across India — clinics, coaching institutes, schools, CA and law firms, real estate and local shops — and international small businesses, startups and agencies who want quality work at India rates. See how we work with clients abroad.
