FAQ

Common questions

Do you require full payment upfront?
No. Projects are split into milestones, and we agree the payment schedule in writing before any work begins.
Are your prices inclusive of GST?
Indian prices are shown before GST. Zera Technologies is GST-registered, so 18% GST is added and you receive a proper GST invoice — if your business is GST-registered, you can usually claim it back as input tax credit. International clients outside India are invoiced in USD without Indian GST.
Are these final prices?
They are honest starting points. Every project gets a fixed written quote after a short chat, and that quote doesn't change unless you change the scope.
Do you have prices in rupees?
Yes. Indian businesses pay in ₹: Starter ₹9,999, Business ₹19,999 and Pro ₹34,999, with domain and first-year hosting included. Prices exclude 18% GST (GST invoice provided). Dental clinics use our ZeraDental packages from ₹14,999.
Can I cancel a monthly SEO plan?
Yes. Monthly plans run month to month. You keep everything that was built for you.
Is hosting included?
Yes for India packages: a .in domain and the first year of hosting are included. After that, hosting is part of the Care plan (₹999/month) or about ₹3,000–4,000 a year if you manage it yourself. International clients can choose Website Care at $39/month.
How much does a website cost in India?
For a small business, a fair price for a custom, mobile-first website is ₹10,000–35,000. Cheap template sites cost ₹3,000–15,000 but often lack real SEO and support; agencies charge ₹50,000–1,50,000+. Our packages are ₹9,999, ₹19,999 and ₹34,999.
