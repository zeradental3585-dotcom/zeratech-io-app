Pricing FAQ

Why do prices auto-convert to my currency?

We work with clients across India and internationally, so prices are set in USD and converted live to your local currency using your approximate location, so you always see a realistic number without doing the math yourself.

Do you require payment upfront?

Most projects are split into milestones. We'll agree on a payment schedule during the scoping call before any work begins.

What if my project doesn't fit these packages?

Most don't fit perfectly. Send us a message on WhatsApp or the contact form and we'll put together a custom quote.
