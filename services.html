FAQ

Common questions

Do you use WordPress or templates?
We hand-code most sites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript so they load fast and are cheap to host. If you need WordPress, Shopify or a specific platform, tell us and we'll quote for it.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We keep your existing URLs (or set up proper redirects) so you don't lose the Google rankings you already have.
Do you write the content?
Yes, in English or Hindi. You review and approve all text before launch.
Is SEO included with a website?
Every site launches with SEO essentials: titles, descriptions, sitemap, schema markup, speed and Search Console setup. Ongoing growth (new pages, content, links) is our monthly SEO plan.
