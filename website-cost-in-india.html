How much does a website cost in India in 2026?

Short answer: a small-business website in India costs anywhere from ₹3,000 to ₹1,50,000+. A fair price for a custom, mobile-first site that actually brings enquiries is about ₹10,000–35,000, plus ₹3,000–8,000 a year to keep it online. Here is what decides where you land — and what to avoid.

So when comparing quotes, ask: what will I pay in year two? A "₹4,999 website" that renews hosting at ₹800 a month can cost more over three years than a ₹15,000 site with cheap hosting.

What actually changes the price

  • Number of pages. Each service, course or treatment page takes writing and design. Five pages and twelve pages are different jobs.
  • Template vs custom design. Templates are cheap but look like thousands of other sites and are often slow.
  • Features. Online registration, fee payment, booking calendars and logins add real work.
  • Languages. A Hindi + English site is close to two sites' worth of text.
  • Who writes the content. If you supply text and photos, it's cheaper; if the developer writes it, it's better for SEO.
  • Support after launch. The biggest hidden difference between a ₹5,000 and a ₹25,000 website.

7 red flags in a website quote

  1. No written list of pages and features.
  2. The developer registers the domain in their name, not yours.
  3. Hosting is "free" but locked to them, with no login for you.
  4. No mention of mobile speed or Google Search Console.
  5. "SEO included" with no explanation of what that means.
  6. 100% payment upfront. A fair split is 50% to start, 50% on handover.
  7. No answer to "who fixes it if it breaks in six months?"

How much should your business spend?

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a basic website cost in India?
A basic 5-page business website costs roughly ₹3,000–15,000 from template sellers and cheap freelancers, ₹10,000–40,000 from a good freelancer and ₹50,000+ from an agency. A fair price for a custom, mobile-first 5-page site with real SEO is around ₹10,000–25,000.
What is the yearly cost of running a website in India?
Domain and hosting together usually cost ₹3,000–8,000 a year. If you want someone to maintain it, monthly care plans typically cost ₹1,000–5,000.
Is Wix or WordPress cheaper than hiring a developer?
Only in the first year. Paid builder plans cost roughly ₹6,000–18,000 every year, forever, and you do the work yourself. Over three to five years a one-time custom site is often cheaper.
Is GST charged on website development?
Yes, if the developer is GST-registered, 18% GST is added to Indian invoices. A GST-registered business can usually claim it back as input tax credit, so ask for a proper GST invoice.
How long does it take to build a business website?
A 5-page site takes about one week, a 10–12 page site about two weeks, and a site with online registration or payments about three weeks.
