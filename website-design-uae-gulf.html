FAQ

Common questions

Do you work during Gulf business hours?
Yes. India is only 1.5 hours ahead of the UAE and Oman and 2.5 hours ahead of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait, so our working day overlaps almost completely with yours. Most clients talk to us on WhatsApp during the day.
How do I pay from the UAE or another Gulf country?
Prices are in US dollars and paid in milestones — usually 50% to start and 50% on handover, after you approve the design. The payment method is agreed in writing before work begins. AED prices on this page are approximate conversions.
Can you build an Arabic version of my website?
Yes, Arabic pages can be added on request and are quoted separately, because they need professional translation and a right-to-left layout.
Will my website show on Google Maps in Dubai or Muscat?
We set up or improve your Google Business Profile, connect it to your website and build pages for your services and area, which helps you appear in local and “near me” searches.
Who owns the website and domain?
You do. The domain, hosting account and code are in your name once the project is paid, and we hand over every login.
