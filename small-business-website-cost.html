How much does a small business website cost in 2026?

Short answer: most small businesses spend $500–5,000 with a freelancer or $3,000–15,000 with an agency for a 5–10 page website, plus $200–600 a year to keep it online. DIY builders look cheaper but charge every month, forever. Here's the full breakdown — and how to pay less without ending up with a bad site.

The question to ask every provider: "What will I pay in year two and year three?" A cheap build with expensive hosting, or a DIY plan you outgrow, often costs more over three years than a fairly priced custom site.

What actually drives the price

  • Pages and content. 5 pages and 15 pages are different projects, especially if the developer writes the copy.
  • Custom design vs template. Custom looks distinctive and loads faster; templates are cheaper.
  • Features. Booking, payments, member areas and integrations add real work.
  • Who you hire. Agency overheads (offices, sales, account managers) are in the price.
  • Support after launch. The cheapest builds usually include none.

How to pay less without getting a bad website

  1. Write a one-page brief: pages, features, examples you like, deadline.
  2. Ask for a fixed written quote with the page list and revision rounds.
  3. Check live work — open the sites on your phone and test the speed.
  4. Pay in milestones (for example 50% to start, 50% on delivery).
  5. Make sure the domain, hosting account and code are in your name.
  6. Consider a capable offshore studio — but only one that meets points 2–5.

FAQ

Common questions

How much should a small business pay for a website in 2026?
Most small businesses pay $500–5,000 with a freelancer or $3,000–15,000 with a small agency for a 5–10 page website. DIY builders cost roughly $200–600 a year in subscriptions plus your own time. Offshore studios with published prices charge less, typically a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars.
What does a website cost per month to run?
Hosting is usually $10–50 a month and a domain $10–20 a year. A maintenance plan that covers updates, backups and small edits commonly costs $50–500 a month depending on the provider and the site.
How much does a website cost in the UK?
In the UK a basic 5-page brochure site from a freelancer is typically £500–1,500, a 5–10 page site £1,000–3,000, and agency brochure sites £3,000–12,000 (more in London). Many quotes exclude 20% VAT.
Is it safe to hire a web developer in India?
It can be, if the provider shows live work you can test, gives a fixed written quote, works in milestones (for example 50% upfront, 50% on delivery), signs an NDA if you need one and hands over all code and logins. Avoid anyone who won't put scope and price in writing.
Is Wix or Squarespace cheaper than hiring a developer?
In year one, usually yes. Over three to five years the subscriptions add up, you spend your own time building and updating, and moving away later often means a full rebuild.
