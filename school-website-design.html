FAQ

Common questions

Do CBSE schools need to publish information on their website?
Yes. CBSE asks affiliated schools to publish a mandatory public disclosure on their website — details such as affiliation, infrastructure, staff and fee information. We set up this section in a clear, easy-to-update format. Please check the latest CBSE circular for the exact list your school must publish.
Can parents check notices and holidays on the website?
Yes. A notice board for circulars, holidays, exam dates and events can be updated from a Google Sheet, so your office staff don't need technical skills.
Can we show the fee structure?
Yes — class-wise fees as a table or downloadable PDF, or a 'request fee details' button if you prefer parents to contact you.
How much does a school website cost?
Starter ₹9,999, Business ₹19,999 (most schools choose this: up to 12 pages, notices from a Google Sheet, Hindi + English) and Pro ₹34,999 (online admission form and fee payment). Domain and first-year hosting are included. Prices exclude 18% GST (GST invoice provided). Try the cost calculator.
Can the website be in Hindi?
Yes — Hindi, English or both, which suits many Bihar Board and Hindi-medium schools.
