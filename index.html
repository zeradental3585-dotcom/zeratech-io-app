How much does a website cost with Zera Technologies?
Business websites start at $249 (up to 5 pages). A 12-page business site with a blog is $599, and e-commerce or booking sites start at $1,299. Indian dental clinic websites start at ₹15,000 through ZeraDental.in. Apps start at $1,799 and monthly SEO starts at $129.
Can I see work you have built?
Yes. Every site in our portfolio is live and was built and is run by Zera Technologies, including BighaWala.com, KosiEducationTimes.com, AivantisGlobal.com, CalcForMoney.com and Filezy.app. Open any of them on your phone and test the speed yourself.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes. We work with small businesses, startups and agencies in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the Gulf. Communication is by email with written updates, and pricing is in US dollars. See how we work.
Do you offer white-label development for agencies?
Yes. Agencies can resell our websites under their own brand. We sign an NDA if needed and hand over all code and logins. See white-label web development.
Who owns the website after it is built?
You do. Once the project is paid for, the domain, code and content belong to you.
What happens after my website goes live?
You can choose a monthly plan for SEO, updates and care so the site keeps working and keeps bringing enquiries, or manage it yourself with full access.
