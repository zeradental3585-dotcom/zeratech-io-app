FAQ

Common questions

How much time overlap do we get with the UK?
India is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of the UK in winter (GMT) and 4 hours 30 minutes ahead in summer (BST). Your morning is our afternoon, so a brief sent by 10am UK time usually gets a reply the same day, and work progresses while you sleep.
Will you ever contact our client?
No. We work only with you, under your brand. We sign an NDA on request, use your naming on files and staging links, and never put our name on the client's site.
How are prices and payments handled?
Fixed quotes in US dollars; pound amounts on this page are approximate (1 GBP ≈ 1.30 USD). Payments are milestone-based and the method is agreed in writing before work starts.
Can we trial you before sending client work?
Yes. We'll build your first landing page at cost, so you can judge code quality, communication and turnaround with almost no risk.
What do you hand over?
Clean, hand-written HTML/CSS/JS (or your preferred stack on quote), all assets, deployment to your hosting or ours, and every login. You own everything once paid.
