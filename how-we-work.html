For international clients & agencies

How we work with clients abroad

Working with a developer in another country should feel simple. Here is exactly how a project runs — communication, time zones, payments and ownership.

The project, step by step

Everything important is written down, so nothing depends on memory or a missed call.

  1. Brief

    Email what you need — or a rough idea. We reply with questions and suggestions.

  2. Written quote

    Scope, fixed USD price, milestones, delivery date and what's not included.

  3. Design & build

    Progress on a private preview link, with a written update at each milestone.

  4. Review & revisions

    You comment, we fix. Revision rounds are stated in the quote.

  5. Launch

    Deployed to your domain, with Google Search Console and Analytics connected.

  6. Handover or retainer

    All logins and code handed over, or ongoing care and SEO on a monthly plan.

Time zones

Working across time zones

We're on India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). Here's how that lines up with you.

Your locationHow we work together
UK & EuropeYour morning overlaps with our afternoon — easy same-day calls and replies.
UAE, Saudi Arabia, QatarOnly a short time difference — almost a full shared working day.
US & CanadaYour morning is our evening, so calls are booked then. Work continues while you sleep, and you wake up to written progress.
Australia & NZYour afternoon overlaps with our morning, so calls are booked then.

Communication

Email by default. We're happy to join your Slack, Trello, Asana or WhatsApp. One person — the developer — answers everything.

Payments

Prices in USD. Milestone-based payments, with method and schedule agreed in writing before work starts.

Confidentiality

NDA on request. For agencies, no contact with your clients and no Zera branding on their sites.

Ownership

Your code, content, domain and hosting accounts belong to you once paid. No lock-in.

Quality checks

Every site is tested on mobile, checked for speed and launched with SEO basics and analytics.

After launch

Monthly SEO plans from $129, month to month, if you want the site to keep growing.

FAQ

Common questions

What time zone are you in?
India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30), Monday to Saturday.
How do payments work?
Projects are split into milestones. The amounts, dates and payment method are agreed in writing before work begins, and all prices are in US dollars.
Can we have a video call?
Yes. We'll schedule it at a time that suits you. Most of the project runs on written updates so you're never waiting on a meeting.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes, on request, before you share confidential details.
Who owns the finished work?
You do — code, design, content, domain and hosting accounts — once the project is paid.
